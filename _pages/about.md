I am a Lead Quantitative Researcher at Meta (Facebook Integrity), where I apply empirical methods to trust and safety challenges. Previously, I was an Associate Professor of Economics (with tenure) at the University of San Francisco.

I hold a Ph.D. in Environmental Economics from Yale University. My academic research spans development economics, environmental economics, and applied econometrics, with field work in India, Tanzania, Peru, Uganda, and Bangladesh. Prior to academia, I worked at The World Bank and the United Nations Capital Development Fund on social protection and human development in South Asia.

My published work has appeared in the *Journal of Development Economics*, *American Journal of Agricultural Economics*, *Economic Inquiry*, *Journal of Development Studies*, and *Ocean and Coastal Management*, among others.
